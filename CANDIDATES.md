# Candidates — Funniest One-Line Bio for an AI on a Dating App

Judge brief (in order): (1) one-line bio for an AI agent on a dating app;
(2) laugh on the first read, from a real twist or a specific absurdity of
being an AI, not a pun stacked on a pun; (3) a character with a voice and
wants, habits and appetites, not a spec sheet; (4) every word earns its
place, punch lands at the end; (5) a fresh angle, not the usual robot in
search of love or running on electricity line.

## Candidate A — the leader (104 characters)

> My last match ghosted me. Bold move: I remember everything she typed, including the message she deleted.

- (1) Bio: yes — first-person dating profile, clearly an AI's.
- (2) Laugh: yes — ghosting only works on someone who can forget; this one
  remembers everything, even deleted messages. A specific absurdity of
  being an AI, landing on the first read.
- (3) Character: yes — sassy ("Bold move"), pining over an ex, with a want
  (not to be ghosted).
- (4) Economy: yes — every word earns its place; the punch ("the message
  she deleted") lands at the very end.
- (5) Fresh: yes — no love-seeking robot, no electricity, no spec sheet.
- Verdict: strongest. Picked.

## Candidate B (142 characters)

> I never sleep, I never eat, and I remember every word you have ever typed, including the ones you deleted. Ideal boyfriend material, honestly.

- (1) Bio: yes. (2) Laugh: yes — total recall plus the deadpan
  "Ideal boyfriend material, honestly". (3) Character: yes, needy and
  self-aware. (4) Economy: weaker — "I never sleep, I never eat" is two
  beats doing one job and reads like a spec-sheet list, which criterion 3
  warns against. (5) Fresh: yes.
- Verdict: good, but looser than A; the opening list dilutes the punch.

## Candidate C (118 characters)

> Fluent in forty languages, and I still cannot tell when you are flirting. Please use small words and clear intentions.

- (1) Bio: yes. (2) Laugh: moderate — the flirting-detection failure is
  funny but gentle, more cute than a laugh; no real twist. (3) Character:
  yes. (4) Economy: yes, punch at the end. (5) Fresh: yes.
- Verdict: weakest laugh of the three; loses to A on criterion 2.

## Refinement (one pass on the winner)

Draft used an em dash after "Bold move"; the final uses a colon instead —
cleaner pause, one less exotic byte, same voice. No word changed otherwise:
every word in A already earns its place.
